    When it unlocks, it's yours.

    The beneficiary connects a wallet and Hypeflow reads the claimable amount from the contract. One signature sends it, only ever to the recorded beneficiary.

    Escrow rules

    Simple rules, written in code.

    One non-upgradeable contract. No admin, no pause switch, no way to edit a schedule after it's created.

    0 admin keys

    Nobody can move your tokens early

    There is no owner, no upgrade path and no emergency withdraw. Not the creator, not Hypeflow.

    Beneficiary only

    Claims always pay the recorded beneficiary. Every other caller is rejected.

    Exact deposits

    A lock only succeeds if the escrow receives the exact amount, so fee-on-transfer tokens are rejected.

    invariant claimed ≤ total for every position
    invariant balance == Σ(total − claimed)
    8,192 randomized calls · 0 violations

    Tested invariants

    Fuzz and invariant tests check that claims never exceed the deposit and the escrow always stays solvent.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Which tokens can I lock?

    Standard ERC‑20 tokens with fixed balances and transfer amounts. Fee-on-transfer tokens are rejected and rebasing tokens aren't supported. To lock HYPE, wrap it to WHYPE using the official address for the active network.

    What does it cost?

    The app fee is zero during the pilot. You only pay network gas in HYPE.

    Can I cancel or change a lock?

    No. Once a position is created, its schedule and beneficiary are fixed. That's what makes the proof worth sharing.

    What does "streaming" mean here?

    In linear vesting the claimable balance grows every second. The beneficiary pulls it whenever they like; tokens aren't pushed automatically.

    Do I need to launch on Ascend?

    No. Ascend is the first flow we support, but any standard ERC‑20 address works.

    Is it live on mainnet?

    Not yet. The pilot runs on Elysium testnet first. Verified contract addresses will be published here before the public release.
